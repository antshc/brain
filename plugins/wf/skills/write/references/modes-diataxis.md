# Pick the mode first (Diátaxis)

Use one mode per whole document; split and link mixed modes. Skip mode selection for short text.

| Purpose | Learning | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Action | Tutorial | How-to |
| Understanding | Explanation | Reference |

- **Tutorial.** Say what the learner will build. Use concrete "we" commands. Every step gives an early visible result and states what to expect. Limit explanation to one clause and a link.
- **How-to.** Solve the reader's problem. Assume competence; give actions, forks and judgment. Link background instead of teaching it. Name the guide by the task.
- **Reference.** Describe facts, options, limits and errors. Keep it dry, complete and precise; no instruction, persuasion or opinion. Mirror the thing described, put facts where readers expect them and generate from code where possible.
- **Explanation.** Answer one real why question, readable away from the product. Give history, constraints, decisions and alternatives; weigh tradeoffs. Titles should fit "About…". Opinion belongs here.

Keep reference tables out of tutorials, teaching out of reference and argument out of how-tos.
