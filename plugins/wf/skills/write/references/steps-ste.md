# Make statements load one at a time (STE rules)

- Give each instruction or thought its own sentence.
- Split instructions beyond about 20 words and other sentences beyond about 25.
- Put the warning or condition before the step it guards.
- Keep articles: "Remove the backup file" has one reading.
- Give each word one meaning and job. Use one word per action: "start" throughout, never alternate with "initiate".
- Write procedures as direct commands, never narration or passive.
- Avoid "-ing" words where possible.
