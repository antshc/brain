# Leave no sentence open to two readings (Global English)

- Put "only" and "not" next to what they change: "only fails on growth" differs from "fails only on growth".
- Break up long noun strings.
- Give "it", "they" and "this" one obvious referent; repeat the noun if needed. Do not make "this" or "which" refer to a whole clause.
- Keep verbs in every clause and structural words such as "that". Never trade clarity for word count.
- Repeat articles when needed: "the client and the host" names two things.
- Make "and" and "or" groupings explicit; use "both…and", "either…or" or "if…then" when needed.
- Use periods instead of semicolons or em dashes. Parentheses contain a grammatical unit or become a sentence. Avoid "(s)" and slashes; write "a, b, or both".
- Keep one name per thing; preserve unchanged terminology.
- Avoid idioms, colloquialisms, Latin abbreviations and metaphors.
