# Research document template

Render this block; remove placeholders. Conclusions summarize integrated evidence. References link independently readable reports. Use the writing-style Bottom line procedure through the living-document instructions.

```markdown
# [Research] <title>

## Bottom line

- **<finding>** <why it matters> <action or status>

## What we did

- <subject>: <verification method, environment, result and limits>

## Open questions / decisions

- <human decision, or none; link related root unknown IDs>

## Remaining work

- <unproven claim and required verification, or no remaining work>

## Next steps

1. <action or handoff>

## References

- [<research topic report>](../research/<slug>/<topic>.md)
- [<experiment report>](../prototype/<slug>/<experiment>/report.md)
```
