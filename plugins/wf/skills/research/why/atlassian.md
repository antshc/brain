# Atlassian

Why investigator source for Jira and Confluence. Apply the Investigator brief; read only your product's section and search only that product.

*Load and follow the `atlassian` skill to search and read Jira or Confluence content in read-only mode*, reusing the router-supplied Preflight result. Start from known keys and links; then search with the anchor's work item IDs, PR references, feature names, symbols, and business terms in several phrasings. Read every selected item in full with comments and revisions; a search snippet is not the item.

## Jira

Work items and decisions. Include parents, linked items, and change history. Cite key + URL, statement or comment location, author/date, the decision, and later revisions.

## Confluence

Designs, ADRs, and HLDs. Cite page URL/title + section or comment, author/date, stated motivation, alternatives considered, and later changes.
